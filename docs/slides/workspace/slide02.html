The Business Problem

Meet Sarah, Regional Merchandising Manager. She's planning a seasonal endcap promotion and needs to:

  • Test the strategy before rollout — simulate shopper behavior and estimate ROI across 3 stores
  • Ensure brand compliance — verify the promotion follows brand guidelines exactly
  • Generate marketing materials — create product imagery that matches brand standards
  • Analyze historical data — understand which products drive revenue in each store
  • Protect customer data — ensure loyalty tier analytics don't expose PII
  • Share the strategy — let other teams discover and reuse her simulation agent
